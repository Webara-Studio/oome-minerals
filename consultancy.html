Division one

Mining consultancy
& transaction advisory.

Practical support for mining operators, mineral suppliers, investors and qualified international counterparties engaging Ghana’s gold sector.

The role

Commercial intelligence that remains grounded on the ground.

OOME supports clients who require a clearer route through Ghanaian mineral-sector realities: relationship-driven operating environments, documentation standards, sourcing questions and transaction readiness.

Advisory work is scoped through a defined engagement. Where OOME may later have a commercial interest in a transaction, that interest should be disclosed and decision rights kept clear.

Commercial readiness

Assess practical requirements, project context, buyer expectations and market pathways before capital or reputation is committed.

Sourcing & responsible-trade frameworks

Support for supplier-verification methods, documentation expectations and evidence-led sourcing processes.

Transaction preparation

Structured preparation around product specification, assay process, commercial terms and supporting documentation.

Counterparty diligence coordination

Organised screening and evidence gathering for serious suppliers, buyers and professional advisers.

Engagement model

Defined scope. Measurable output.

OOME’s consultancy is designed to be commercially useful, not academic. Assignments should result in clearer decisions, a documented readiness position or a controlled next step.
