Our position

Responsible trade is a commercial advantage.

OOME’s dealing and export model is designed around documented product specifications, defined commercial terms and appropriate checks. Any principal dealing or export activity is undertaken only where OOME holds the required confirmed authority and transaction approvals.

This public site does not advertise gold stock, prices, delivery times or export assurances. Those matters are considered only after counterparty review and within the applicable legal and regulatory perimeter.

1. Initial qualification

OOME reviews the organisation, jurisdiction, commercial purpose, intended product requirements and initial volume indication.

2. Counterparty review

Corporate identity, beneficial ownership, sanctions exposure and other relevant diligence are considered before substantive engagement.

3. Transaction definition

Where appropriate, product, assay, pricing method, delivery point, documentation and settlement conditions are defined.

4. Controlled execution

Only approved transactions proceed, with a documented deal file and relevant approvals, logistics and settlement controls.
