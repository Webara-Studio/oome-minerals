Replace this host consistently in canonical tags, Open Graph/Twitter URLs, JSON-LD, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` once an approved custom domain is live.

## Search Console
1. Sign in using the organisation-controlled Google account.
2. Add the exact URL-prefix property: `https://oome-minerals.vercel.app/`.
3. Verify through the recommended method available to the account.
4. Submit `https://oome-minerals.vercel.app/sitemap.xml`.
5. Inspect the homepage and the two principal commercial pages after publication.

## Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Sign in using the organisation-controlled Microsoft account.
2. Add `https://oome-minerals.vercel.app/`.
3. Verify ownership through the approved method.
4. Submit the same sitemap.

## Before connecting a custom domain
- Confirm domain ownership and HTTPS on the intended Vercel project.
- Choose one canonical form (`www` or apex) and redirect the other permanently.
- Replace every Vercel-alias reference in public SEO files as one release.
- Add the custom host to Search Console and Bing, then resubmit the sitemap.

## Measurement launch gate
Analytics and conversion tracking are not yet installed because no OOME-controlled analytics property or consent policy has been approved. Add these only with an organisation-controlled account, privacy notice and an agreed enquiry conversion definition.

## Content programme
Prioritise factual authority material rather than generic keyword pages:
- Responsible gold-trade engagement and buyer qualification.
- Ghana mining transaction readiness and documentation discipline.
- Counterparty diligence and responsible sourcing principles.
- Compliance-approved market insights relevant to qualified B2B audiences.

Do not publish commodity prices, supply claims, licence claims, deal examples or regulatory guidance without evidence and legal/compliance approval.
